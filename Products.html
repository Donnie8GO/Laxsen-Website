Shutters

Window Shutters are the most energy efficient, child friendly, UV protective product on the market. They are known for their elegant appearance, choosing the right shutter will add a great value to any home. We guarantee the compounds will be free of warpage, cracking, and discoloration from the sun and are totally waterproof. Shutters provide an extra layer of insulation, allowing you to more efficiently heat or cool your home. Vinyl-Tek Shutters & Blinds has an innovative frame system. All 4-sided frames are accompanied by a catch-less receiver which eliminates the use of magnets. There are many type of shutters on the market such as Vinyl, Wood, Polywood and Aluminum shutters.

Vinyl Shutters

Interior vinyl shutters give you the ability to open, close and adjust them as necessary. You can get these shutters in virtually any style and colour. They are incredibly easy to clean. Vinyl shutters are made of lightweight and durable strips of vinyl. The louvers on these shutters range from hollow to solid. Offered in 2 1/2 & 3 1/2 & 4 1/2 in louvred.

Blinds & Shades

Both window blinds and window shades deliver the benefits of blocking out light and giving you privacy. We carry a diverse collection of roller blinds, zebra blinds, silhouette blinds, cellular blinds, faux wood blinds, pleated shades, vertical shades and others. They give you the option of choosing the light filtering or room darkening materials to allow the exact amount of light that you want.

Vertical Blinds

Vertical blinds are very good at controlling how much natural or exterior light comes into a room, due to the ability of the slats to close tightly. Giving Protection against cracking, chipping, discolouration, peeling or warping. Easy Maintenance, No cords, Clean look on panel and top & bottom frames, Will not get mold, mildew, or absorb moisture and helps Save on air conditioning & heating bills
